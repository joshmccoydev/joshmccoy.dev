How I build

Same as a job site. Measure twice, build what's needed, clean up after.

  1. 01

    Look at the job first

    I watch how the work gets done today before I write anything. Most of the fix is in that first look.

  2. 02

    Build the smallest thing that works

    One tool, one job. It ships early, gets used and gets better from real use.

  3. 03

    Keep a human on the switch

    Bots draft and prepare. People approve anything that sends, posts or spends money.
