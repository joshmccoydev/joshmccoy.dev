How I build

Same rules as a job site. Look twice, build what the job needs, leave it clean.

  1. 01

    Look at the job first

    I watch how the work gets done before I write anything. Most of the answer is in that first look.

  2. 02

    Build the smallest thing that works

    One tool, one job. It ships while it's still small, then it gets better because someone is using it.

  3. 03

    Keep a human on the switch

    Bots draft and prepare. A person approves anything that sends, posts, or spends money.
