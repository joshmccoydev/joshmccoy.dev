OwnerMathSheets

Spreadsheet templates for people who run the place.

Three sheets: a plate cost calculator, a bakery pricing calculator, and a weekly food cost tracker. Excel and Google Sheets.

They are not public yet. There is no file to download, and no sample menu from a real kitchen on this page.

The idea is a sheet that refuses a blank cost instead of pretending it is zero.

CellWhat it does
I5The cost, if someone typed one
formula=IF(ISNUMBER(I5),I5,NA())

A blank stays blank. It does not become zero.
