Work · 01

Bot team

Assistants with one job each. They draft. I approve.

I got tired of one chat doing every job and nobody knowing what had been sent. The fix was the same as a crew: clear jobs, written rules, and a person on the switch.

A chief of staff hands work between them. Sales drafts, an editor cleans them up, research checks the source, code ships, and the day (inbox, calendar, notes) stays in one place. Anything that sends, posts, or spends waits for me.

The full study, in my own words, is on j0sh.wtf: how i run my work.
