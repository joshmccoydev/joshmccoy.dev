Spreadsheets for people who run the place

Plate cost, bakery pricing, and what the food cost did this week.

The owners I teach rarely need a new app first. They need a number they trust: what a plate costs, what the bakery item should sell for, whether food cost drifted this week.

OwnerMathSheets is those three sheets, in Excel and Google Sheets. If a formula can't be read out loud, it doesn't stay.

Still launching. I made it for people who run a kitchen.
